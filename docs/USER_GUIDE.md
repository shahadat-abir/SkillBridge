# User Guide — SkillBridge

## Getting started
1. Open the site and choose **Join SkillBridge**. Pick **Hunter** (find work) or **Employer** (hire).
2. Sign in. Your **Dashboard** shows your current numbers and next actions. The bell shows unread notifications.

## Hunters
1. **Profile → Edit profile**: add a headline, about, education, experience, portfolio link and availability.
2. **My skills**: add each skill with your level for *that* skill. Levels are self-reported and shown to employers as such.
3. **Browse jobs**: filter by keyword, category, skill, difficulty (stars), mode, budget and status.
4. Open a job to see the full requirements and your skill match. Click **Apply / submit a bid**, write a proposal, set price and days.
5. Follow your applications in **My applications** (Submitted → Under review → Shortlisted → Accepted/Rejected). You may withdraw an active application.
6. When selected, open the assignment and **Accept** (or Decline).
7. **Submit work** with a description and a file and/or link. If the employer requests a revision, read the feedback and submit a new version.
8. After approval the job is completed; check **Payments** for the ledger status and leave a rating for the employer.

### Challenges
Challenge jobs are done by several Hunters at once. Read the rules box, tick the confirmation and **Join challenge**.
Submit before the deadline. Submissions are reviewed in the order received; the first one that passes review wins the reward.

## Employers
1. **Post a job**: fill in details, budget, payment type, difficulty, required skills with minimum levels, deadlines and instructions. Choose *Challenge* mode to let several Hunters attempt the same task.
2. **My jobs** lists your jobs. Use **Compare applicants** to see candidates side by side. Highlights are informational; you decide.
3. Mark applicants Under review / Shortlist / Reject, then **Select** one. The Hunter must accept.
4. When work is submitted, **Approve completion** or **Request revision** (a reason is required).
5. Approved payments appear in **Payments**: choose **Record payment** after you have paid the Hunter outside SkillBridge (simulated ledger).
6. Leave a 1–5 star rating for the Hunter.
7. For challenges use the **Challenge review board**: decide on the earliest pending submission (passes → winner; fails → next submission).

## Admins
**Admin** menu: overview, users (set role, suspend/reactivate), jobs (cancel with reason), categories & skills, reports, payment ledger and audit log.
